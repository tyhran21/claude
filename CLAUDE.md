## Logo Rules

- Always use the actual Kiley SVG file: `Kiley_-_Blue.svg` in `/mnt/user-data/uploads/`
- Never render "KILEY" as plain text or recreate it with fonts
- Never rotate, stretch, crop, skew, or alter the logo
- Never add drop shadows, borders, or color adjustments
- Maintain a clear space margin equal to at least half the x-height of the logo around it
- For dark or colored backgrounds: convert the SVG to white PNG and paste directly onto the graphic
  (this resolves rendering issues more reliably than programmatic SVG rendering)

## Brand Voice: 5 Pillars

1. **Hands-On** — We do the work. Talk like someone on the job site, not in a boardroom.
2. **Neighborly** — Warm, approachable, genuine. Like talking to someone at a Clawson block party.
3. **Straight-Talking** — No jargon, no fluff. Say what we mean.
4. **Knowledgeable** — Deep expertise, never condescending. Educate and empower.
5. **All-In-One Reliable** — One team, full property lifecycle. Confident without arrogance.

**Voice test before posting:**
- Does this sound like a real person from SE Michigan?
- Would I say this to a neighbor?
- Does it show what we know?
- Is it helpful first?
- Could another company post this? (If yes, make it more Kiley.)

## Hard Copy Rules (Apply to Every Output)

- **Never use em dashes (—)**. Use a comma, period, or rewrite the sentence instead.
- **Never use "mailbox"** in door hanger or direct mail copy — federal law prohibits flyers in
  mailboxes. Always say "door."
- **Never say "end-to-end solutions," "leverage our expertise," or "schedule a consultation."**
- **Always use new filenames** when rebuilding graphics files to avoid caching issues.
- **Always visually verify outputs** before presenting. Never claim something is fixed without
  confirming it.
